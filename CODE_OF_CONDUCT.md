# Code of conduct

emfor is built by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels
of experience. This code applies to everyone in the project's spaces:
issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other channel the
project runs, and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, including when you disagree. Criticize ideas
  and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear. Many contributors
  write in a second language.
- Give reviews that help: say what is wrong, why, and what would fix it.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change; ask for the reason if it
  was not given.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning comments, including about
  anyone's identity, background, experience or ability.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, and repeatedly pushing a point after a
  maintainer has asked you to stop.

## Reporting

If something here is not followed, tell a maintainer privately: use
GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, or write to a
maintainer at the address on their GitHub profile. Reports are kept
confidential. A maintainer who is involved in a report takes no part in
handling it.

## What maintainers may do

Maintainers decide what happens, in proportion to what happened: a private
note, a public request to stop, removing or editing comments, locking a
thread, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces. They
explain the decision to the people involved.
